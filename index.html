    Estimator setup

    A small, constant sensor offset is integrated for as long as the pack runs, so the estimate drifts away from the true charge. A real BMS corrects this at rest from the open-circuit voltage and at the end of a full charge. Raise the simulation speed to watch the drift build up.

    Balancing control

    Strategy

    Passive balancing burns the extra charge of high cells in a 40 Ω resistor (about 100 mA). Active balancing moves 2 A from the highest cell to the lowest with about 90 % efficiency. Balancing runs only when pack current is under 3 A.
